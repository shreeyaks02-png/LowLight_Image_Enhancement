# Failure Case Analysis

## Potential Failure Cases

1. **Image Noise:** Extreme low-light enhancement may increase visible noise.
2. **Colour Distortion:** Brightness adjustment may affect original colour accuracy.
3. **Overexposure:** Strong enhancement may make bright regions excessively bright.
4. **Detail Loss:** Filtering may reduce fine image details.
  
## Conclusion

The system improves low-light image visibility, but noise, colour distortion and detail preservation remain challenges. Further testing and optimization are required.
